comoLer: >-
  Every lifecycle model aligned on its debut week and normalized to its own peak, so 100% is each model's high point, not the market's. The thick line is the median and the band spans the first to third quartiles; the thin curves are individual models, colored by lab origin, and hovering highlights one. The dashed line marks two quarters of age. Two modes: the overall shape, or one small multiple per debut quarter against the overall median, which is where you can see whether seasons are getting shorter.
perguntaQueResponde: >-
  What does a typical season look like? A fast rise, an early peak, a long decline. This is the chart behind the thesis that the durable asset is the method for switching, not the choice of model.
oQueNaoMostra: >-
  Normalizing to each model's own peak erases scale: a model that reached 30% and one that barely cleared the cutoff draw the same curve. It compares shape, not size. It describes the full history and does not respond to the window or filters, and recent cohorts have fewer observed weeks, so the end of their curves is where the data runs out.
