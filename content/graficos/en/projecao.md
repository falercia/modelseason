indicadores:

comoLer: >-
  A table with one row per indicator, no chart. It shows the current value, the per-period pace of the least-squares fitted line and where that line lands at the end of the horizon. When the line hits the floor or ceiling before one and a half times the horizon, the last column says when, in red, instead of the landing value. The baseline and horizon come from the selected window, and the horizon never exceeds a quarter of the observed span.
perguntaQueResponde: >-
  If nothing changes, where will these numbers be a quarter from now? It helps gauge the order of magnitude and prompt the right question.
oQueNaoMostra: >-
  This is not a forecast, and the whole page exists to show that these rates change. When the line hits the floor or ceiling within one and a half times the horizon, the landing value is not published, because by then the line has already broken down.
