comoLer: >-
  One bubble per lab with volume in the latest period, with area proportional to tokens and color by origin. Position is a percentile within the group, not an absolute value. Vertical is traction, combining token share, spend share and growth. Horizontal is capability, in two modes: declared features, which covers every lab, or the Intelligence Index, which covers only labs with an evaluated model. The median lines divide the four quadrants, and the dashed trail shows where the largest labs were a quarter earlier, or at the start of the window if it is shorter. The weight of each component is listed below the chart.
perguntaQueResponde: >-
  Who leads, who challenges, who shows promise and who is niche, on a single screen. The component weights are adjustable, so you can test whether a lab's position hinges on a choice you made.
oQueNaoMostra: >-
  A percentile is a relative position: moving up here can mean the others got worse. And declared capability is what the catalog advertises, not measured performance.
