comoLer: >-
  Bars grouped by launch quarter, two per quarter: the median number of weeks to peak share, and the median number of weeks from the peak until losing half of it. Only models that reached 2% of weekly volume are included, and each cohort's n is in the label, because a cohort of three models does not support the same reading as one of twenty.
perguntaQueResponde: >-
  Is a model's season getting shorter? Comparing cohorts is what separates a trend from the noise of a single launch.
oQueNaoMostra: >-
  It describes the full history and does not respond to the window or grouping. Recent cohorts carry censoring bias: a model that has not yet fallen to half its peak is left out of the half-life median, so the median counts only those that fell quickly and tends to rise. The hollow bar marks these cases. The first cohort includes models that already existed when the series begins.
