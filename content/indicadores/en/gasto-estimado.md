comoECalculado: >-
  Tokens multiplied by each model's price. Because the source sums prompt and completion without splitting them, and completion costs several times more, we publish three numbers: a floor (all prompt), a ceiling (all completion) and the estimate at the declared mix. Free endpoints cost zero.

  It is not anyone's revenue. It ignores discounts, caching, batch pricing, contracts and the fact that part of the traffic runs on promotional credits. Use it to compare positioning across labs, not to size anyone's sales.
