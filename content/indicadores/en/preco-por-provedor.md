---
titulo: "Price by provider"
oQueE: >-
  The same model served by different providers, each with its own price, maximum context, quantization and data retention policy.
comoECalculado: >-
  It comes from the endpoint list in the source's catalog, in dollars per million input and output tokens. Zero data retention flags an endpoint that declares it stores neither prompt nor response. Quantization is the declared numerical precision of the weights being served.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  List price excludes caching, discounts and rate limits. Lower quantization can change response quality, and not every provider declares its own. Latency and availability do not appear here.
