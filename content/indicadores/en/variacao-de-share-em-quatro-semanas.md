---
titulo: "Four-week share change"
oQueE: >-
  The difference, in percentage points, between a model's current share and its share four weeks earlier.
comoECalculado: >-
  Simple subtraction of percentages. When grouped by month, the comparison is with the previous month, the equivalent period.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  A percentage point is not a percent: going from 1% to 2% is +1 pp and also a doubling. For small models, the relative change tells more of the story.
---
