---
titulo: "Token share"
oQueE: >-
  The share of weekly volume captured by each model, lab or category.
comoECalculado: >-
  The entity's tokens divided by the week's total, with the <span class='mono'>other</span> line included in the denominator. With a filter active, the denominator becomes the total of the filtered view.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  It is not market share by revenue or by users. A free model with heavy traffic has a high share and zero revenue. Always compare it with share of spend.
---
