comoECalculado: >-
  The blended price uses the declared mix of prompt and completion. The tiers are fixed cutoffs, published in the code, not rolling quantiles, so that what each tier means does not change from one week to the next.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  List price is not price paid: volume discounts, caching and contracts do not show up. An advertised maximum context also does not guarantee quality across the whole window.
