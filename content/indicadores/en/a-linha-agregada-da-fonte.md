titulo: "The source's aggregate line"
oQueE: >-
  Volume that fell outside each day's top 50, rolled up by the source into a single line.
comoECalculado: >-
  It comes straight from the API. It counts toward the total and the denominator of every percentage, but carries no metadata: we don't know which models it contains.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  That is why it <b>drops out of any filtered view</b>. With a filter active, percentages are calculated within the filtered view, and the filter bar shows how much volume was left out.
