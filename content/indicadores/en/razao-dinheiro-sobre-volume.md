---
titulo: "Money-to-volume ratio"
oQueE: >-
  How much of the money a lab captures relative to how many tokens it processes.
comoECalculado: >-
  Share of estimated spend divided by share of tokens. A ratio of 1 means the lab charges the market's average price; above 1, it charges more; below 1, less.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  It does not measure margin or efficiency. A lab with a high ratio may simply serve more expensive use cases rather than be more profitable.
---
