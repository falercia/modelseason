---
titulo: "Time to peak and half-life"
oQueE: >-
  How many weeks a model takes from launch to its highest share, and how many more it takes afterward to lose half of it.
comoECalculado: >-
  Calculated per model, over the full series. Only models that reached at least 2% of weekly volume are included, so that noise from tiny models does not dominate the median. A model that has not yet fallen to half its peak shows no half-life rather than counting as zero.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  A short half-life is not a sign of a bad model. It is a sign of a market with a fast release cadence, and the effect shows up in good and bad models alike.
