---
titulo: "Daily 7- and 30-day windows"
oQueE: >-
  The sum of daily volume over the last 7 or 30 days the source has published, compared with the immediately preceding window of the same length. It is the measure behind the Now panel.
comoECalculado: >-
  The window ends on the last day the source published, never on today's date. Share is the model's volume divided by the window total, with the <span class='mono'>other</span> line in the denominator. Endpoint variants, such as <span class='mono'>:free</span>, are summed into the base model.
oQueNaoConclui: >-
  Seven days is the most volatile view on the page. A strong week may be a launch spike or test traffic, and it only becomes a trend when it repeats across non-overlapping windows.
